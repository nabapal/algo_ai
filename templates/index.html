CALL/PUT/BOTH are only entered if the VIX-implied expected move clears the relevant side's REAL, live ATM premium (its own breakeven) times this margin - e.g. 1.15 means the move must beat breakeven by 15%. This is never a fixed points number: it's derived fresh from today's actual option price every time, since the same points move means something different on a day the option is cheap vs expensive.
Today's already-realized move (vs today's Open) only counts as directional evidence once it's used up at least this fraction of the VIX-implied expected move - below this, it's treated as normal noise, not a signal.
...and stops counting past this fraction - a move that's already used up most of today's expected range is more likely near-exhausted than about to extend, so it's deliberately excluded rather than counted as extra-strong evidence (avoids chasing).
Resistance/Support walls are also weighted by whether their OI is fresh build-up or unwinding since yesterday's close (not just today's raw size). This caps how much a single day's OI swing can strengthen/weaken a wall - default 0.5 means at most ±50%, so one unusual day can't dominate the whole read.
Confidence = (expected move ÷ recent avg range) × this. E.g. 50 means a "typical" day (ratio 1.0) scores 50%, and double-the-typical-range day scores 100%.
Flat points added if today's price has already broken out past today's own opening-range high/low - a real, confirmed move, not just an implied one.
Max points added based on how much of today's expected move is already realized (scales from 0 up to this value as more of the range gets used).
Max points added when the two nearest OI walls show heavy fresh build-up or unwinding since yesterday (either direction - real option-chain positioning, not just VIX). Scales up to this value at the OI build-up/unwind clamp above.
Max points added when yesterday's Central Pivot Range is narrow (close sat near the middle of its own day's range) - historically precedes a bigger move. Scales down to 0 as the CPR widens.
Mode is set by dry_run in settings.json and is intentionally not editable from this page — see README for how to go live.
